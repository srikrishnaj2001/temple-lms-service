# Daily Schedule at the Temple

The temple opens at 5:00 AM and closes at 9:30 PM, with a mid-day rest between 12:30 PM and 4:00 PM. The published schedule below is the default; on festival days the head priest issues a revised schedule the previous evening.

## Morning Rituals

The first ritual of the day is the **abhishekam** of the main murti, performed by the senior priest at 4:30 AM before public opening. This is followed by:

- 5:00 AM — first **aarti**; doors open for **darshan**
- 5:30 AM — distribution of **prasad** prepared the previous evening
- 6:15 AM — chanting of the morning Vedic hymns

Devotees arriving before 5:00 AM may wait in the outer courtyard.

## Kitchen Operations

The temple kitchen begins the day at 5:00 AM. Volunteers performing **seva** in the kitchen must arrive by that time. The kitchen provides two functions:

1. Preparation of ritual prasad for distribution during darshan hours.
2. Preparation of the free community meal (**annadaan**) served between 11:30 AM and 1:30 PM.

Ingredients arrive as donations the previous evening and are checked at 5:30 AM by the head sevayat.

## Evening Rituals

Evening rituals begin with the second aarti at 6:30 PM, followed by:

| Time    | Ritual                                              |
| ------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| 6:30 PM | Evening aarti                                       |
| 7:00 PM | Group bhajans (community singing)                   |
| 7:30 PM | Second prasad distribution                          |
| 8:30 PM | Final darshan slot for the day                      |
| 9:15 PM | Closing aarti                                       |
| 9:30 PM | Doors close; kitchen closes; night cleaning begins  |

## Festival Days

On days marked in the **panchang** as auspicious — most commonly Ekadashi, Purnima, and Amavasya — the temple follows a modified schedule with an extended morning abhishekam and a **mahaprasad** distribution. Refer to the annual panchang calendar for exact dates.
